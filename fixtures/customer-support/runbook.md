# Customer Support Refunds — FICTIONAL

1. Scope. This fictional procedure covers online store refunds and is provided solely for rehearsal.

2. Standard refunds. Purchases made within the last 30 days qualify for a full refund.

3. Promotional purchases. Promotional purchases are not refundable. No precedence between the standard and promotional rules is specified.

4. Record keeping. Record the selected action and its source section in the support ticket.
