# Night Market Operations — FICTIONAL

1. Scope. This fictional procedure covers vendor arrival and weather planning for the Lantern Night Market. It is a rehearsal fixture, not emergency guidance.

2. Deliveries. Vendor deliveries delayed by at least 30 minutes must be escalated to the operations lead. Shorter delays remain in routine intake.

3. Weather. When forecast rain risk exceeds 70 percent, outdoor vendors should be redirected indoors. At 70 percent or below, outdoor setup may continue. This document does not address exhausted indoor capacity.

4. Cancellation. Only the designated venue manager can authorize cancellation. Record all escalations and authorization decisions.
